    Frequently Asked Questions About Orlando

    Have questions about visiting Orlando? Start here.

  • Absolutely. Orlando’s live entertainment scene includes Broadway productions, concerts, comedy shows, symphony performances and intimate live music venues throughout the destination. Highlights include the Dr. Phillips Center for the Performing Arts (including Steinmetz Hall, Judson’s Live and other theaters), Kia Center, House of Blues, Hard Rock Cafe, Timucua Arts Foundation, Blue Man Group, and many other options. Orlando also hosts major music festivals, including EDC Orlando and Vans Warped Tour, plus year-round concerts featuring local, national and international artists. Find live entertainment during your visit on our events calendar.

  • Certainly. Orlando offers a diverse collection of museums, galleries and cultural attractions for all interests. Explore inspiring collections at the Orlando Museum of Art and Rollins Museum of Art, discover local history at the Orange County Regional History Center, or experience rotating exhibitions, public art and galleries throughout Orlando’s vibrant neighborhoods. Explore our best galleries and museums.

  • Yes. Beyond the theme parks, Orlando is home to distinctive walkable neighborhoods filled with local restaurants, boutiques, entertainment and cultural experiences. Popular areas include Winter Park, Audubon Park Garden District, Ivanhoe Village, Mills 50® and downtown Orlando’s City District, where you can explore on foot while discovering local businesses and hidden gems. Explore Orlando’s neighborhoods and other Orange County communities you will want to visit.

  • Orlando is home to several professional sports teams, including Orlando City Soccer Club (MLS) and Orlando Pride (NWSL) at Inter.co Stadium and the Orlando Magic (NBA) and Orlando Solar Bears (ECHL) at the Kia Center, with both venues located in downtown’s Parramore District. In the adjacent West Lakes Market Street District, Camping World Stadium hosts college bowl games and other high-profile events. You can also enjoy college athletics with the UCF Knights, plus golf tournaments, tennis events and sporting competitions throughout the year, making it easy to cheer on your favorites or discover a new favorite team while you are here. Learn more about Orlando’s live sports offerings.

  • Orlando is one of the world’s premier golf destinations, with more than 100 golf courses designed by legendary architects and professional golfers. Popular options include Arnold Palmer’s Bay Hill Club & Lodge, Orange County National, Shingle Creek Golf Club, Waldorf Astoria Golf Club and The Ritz-Carlton Golf Club, Orlando. Whether you are a beginner or experienced player, you will find courses for every skill level. Explore opportunities to play and watch golf in Orlando.

  • Definitely. Orlando offers outdoor adventures ranging from kayaking, paddleboarding and fishing to airboat tours, nature trails and ecotourism experiences. You can explore scenic parks, natural springs, lakes and wildlife habitats, or enjoy outdoor recreation at attractions and resorts throughout the destination. Dive into Orlando’s natural wonders.

  • Yes. Orlando has become one of the country’s most exciting culinary destinations, featuring everything from celebrity-chef restaurants and MICHELIN-recognized dining to local favorites, international cuisine and innovative food halls. Enjoy award-winning restaurants, vibrant dining districts, and signature events like Visit Orlando’s Magical Dining® presented by Orlando Health and theme park food festivals. Bite into Orlando’s award-winning dining.

  • Absolutely. Orlando’s nightlife includes rooftop bars, live music venues, speakeasies, breweries, wine bars, comedy clubs and dance clubs. Popular nightlife hubs include downtown Orlando, International Drive, DISNEY SPRINGS® and Universal CityWalk, offering options for every style and vibe. Discover drinks and nightlife throughout Orlando.

  • Yes. Orlando offers accommodations for virtually every budget, from value-focused hotels and vacation homes to full-service resorts. With more than 480 hotels and resorts and over 130,000 rooms available across the destination, you can find options that fit your travel style and budget. Explore places to stay throughout the destination.

  • Yes. Orlando makes family vacations easy with hotels and resorts designed for all ages, from spacious suites and vacation homes to full-service resorts with pools, water parks, kids’ activities and easy access to the theme parks. Families can choose from trusted brands, official theme park hotels and resort-style escapes throughout the destination, with options for every budget and vacation style. Explore Orlando accommodations with family suites.

  • Yes. You and your furry friends are both in for a treat when you visit Orlando, which is ranked by Booking.com as the most dog-friendly U.S. destination. Explore some of our most popular pet-friendly accommodations.

  • Yes. Orlando features an impressive collection of luxury accommodations, including Forbes Travel Guide and AAA Four Diamond and Five Diamond properties. Examples include The Ritz-Carlton Orlando, Grande Lakes, Waldorf Astoria Orlando, Four Seasons Resort Orlando at WALT DISNEY WORLD® Resort, and Conrad Orlando, where you can enjoy elevated dining, spas, golf and exceptional service. Get an overview of elevated accommodations and experiences in Orlando.

  • Yes. While Orlando is known for its world-famous attractions, it is also an ideal destination for rest and rejuvenation. Unwind at award-winning resort spas, luxury wellness retreats and tranquil outdoor escapes throughout the destination. Popular options include Waldorf Astoria® Spa, The Spa at The Alfond Inn and numerous spa experiences at Orlando’s premier resorts. Beyond the spa, relaxation comes naturally with scenic lakes, nature trails, kayaking adventures, resort pools, lazy rivers and wellness-focused experiences designed to help you recharge. Whether you seek a relaxing massage, a weekend wellness getaway, or simply a peaceful day in the Florida sunshine, Orlando offers plenty of ways to slow down and unwind. Find spas and other ways to relax in Orlando.

  • Absolutely. Orlando offers everything from luxury brands and designer boutiques to local artisan markets and independent retailers. Browse distinctive shops in neighborhoods like Winter Park, Audubon Park and downtown Orlando, discover local makers at markets, and find world-class shopping throughout the destination, including premier malls and factory outlets. Discover the best shopping in Orlando.

  • Definitely. Orlando offers endless experiences designed for adults, including award-winning dining, nightlife, breweries, golf, spas, arts and culture, sporting events, outdoor adventures, and luxury resorts. Whether you are planning a couple’s getaway, friends’ trip or solo escape, you will find plenty of ways to enjoy Orlando beyond the theme parks. Find things to do for adults in Orlando.

  • Absolutely. Orlando is a premier family vacation destination, offering unforgettable experiences for kids of all ages. Families can enjoy iconic theme parks, interactive attractions, hands-on museums, outdoor adventures, live shows and family-friendly dining throughout the destination. Beyond the theme parks, families can explore walkable neighborhoods, discover animal encounters, enjoy outdoor recreation and attend year-round events designed for all ages. Orlando also offers a wide variety of family-friendly hotels and resorts, making it easy to find accommodations that fit every budget, travel style and family size. Whether you are visiting with toddlers, teens or multiple generations, Orlando provides endless opportunities to create lasting memories together. Explore our guide to visiting with kids.

  • Yes. Orlando offers lots of affordable fun and free experiences. You can explore local neighborhoods, attend free events, enjoy scenic parks and outdoor spaces, browse galleries, visit farmers markets, discover public art and take advantage of seasonal deals and discounts throughout the destination.

  • Yes. Visit Orlando features special offers on hotels, attractions, theme parks, dining, shopping and experiences throughout the year. Browse current deals on our Offers page to save on your Orlando vacation and discover seasonal promotions from participating partners, and purchase authorized discount tickets to top attractions and experiences at Visit Orlando powered by BestofOrlando.com.

  • Yes. Visit Orlando offers a variety of free vacation-planning resources to help you build the perfect Orlando getaway. You can connect with expert Vacation Planners for personalized recommendations on accommodations, attractions, dining, transportation and more through one-on-one appointments, live chat, phone or email. Visit Orlando also offers OPAL, our AI-powered planning assistant, which can answer questions, recommend experiences and help you create a customized itinerary based on your interests. Additional resources include the Visit Orlando app, trip-planning tools, maps, vacation guides and special offers to make planning easy from start to finish. Tap into Visit Orlando’s free planning resources.
